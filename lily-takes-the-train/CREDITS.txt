OTHER PROJECT COMPONENTS

Story dialogue: supplied by the user.
Narration: generated standard female English voice (en-US-JennyNeural),
using the supplied dialogue; no reference-speaker voice cloning was used.
Home environment, story props, camera sequence, caption assembly, and new
animation assembly: created for this project.

The user's reference video was used to guide the visual presentation and
caption treatment. Its footage and soundtrack were not copied into this
film. The project is a new adaptation in a similar general presentation;
it does not contain the original reference character or its original rig.
